OUR STORY

Rooted in earth.
Shaped by hands.

Pottery is an old conversation between people and the earth. We want to keep that conversation alive in the things we make and use.

Inspired by the familiar clay traditions of Bangladesh, Pottery Shop brings a thoughtful, contemporary sensibility to objects shaped for everyday rituals.

A glimpse into the making process
A BANGLADESHI SENSIBILITY

Everyday objects.
Deep roots.

From the familiar forms of earthenware to contemporary shapes for modern homes, our visual language takes inspiration from the textures, colours, and everyday rhythms of Bangladesh.

We believe good design doesn't need to shout. It should feel considered, useful, and quietly full of character.
